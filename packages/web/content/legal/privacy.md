**Effective Date: 18/08/2025**

## Information We Collect

ZeepCentraal ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use our services.

- Steam SSO (Single Sign-On): Your Steam ID and Steam display name.
  - We respect your Steam profile privacy settings. We can only see and store your Steam display name if it is publicly accessible on Steam.
  - If your Steam profile is set to Hidden or Friends Only, your display name will not be stored. On ZeepCentraal and GTR, you will instead be shown as “Unknown Player”.
- Discord SSO (if manually linked): Your Discord user ID.
- Cookies: Functional cookies are used for authentication (to keep you logged in) and to remember your language preferences.

We do not collect personal information such as your email address, real name, or payment details.

## Data Sources

Data on ZeepCentraal comes from two primary methods:

1. Super League Results
   - By playing in the Zeepkist Super League (ZSL), you agree to have your historical results displayed on ZeepCentraal.
   - If you do not have a ZeepCentraal account, a placeholder account may be created using your publicly accessible Steam display name, solely for the purpose of displaying tournament results.
   - You will not be treated as an active GTR mod user unless you separately install and use the GTR mod.
2. GTR Mod Submissions
   - By downloading and using the GTR mod in Zeepkist, you agree to submit:
     - Level times (your recorded performance).
     - Level hash (to identify which level was played).
     - Ghost data (telemetry such as position, speed, arms up, and other soapbox status indicators).
   - This data is collected to verify the integrity of submitted records and to provide interactive visualisations of runs and related performance metrics on ZeepCentraal.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To authenticate your account via Steam or Discord.
- To link your account with ZeepCentraal features (e.g., gameplay records, rankings, or community features).
- To provide a localized experience (e.g., language preference).
- To make your ZeepCentraal account and gameplay data available via our public GraphQL API, which allows Zeepkist mods, Discord bots, and other tools to interact with ZeepCentraal.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Public Data

Some information is made publicly accessible:

- Your Steam ID, Steam display name (if public), and Discord ID (if linked) may be visible on ZeepCentraal and through the public API.
- If your Steam profile is private or hidden, your identity will be shown as “Unknown Player”.
- Gameplay data such as records, results, and rankings are considered public game performance data and may be displayed on ZeepCentraal, shared in leaderboards, or accessed via the API.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your ZeepCentraal account exists.

- Super League results: Match and tournament results are not deleted, as they form part of the permanent competition history.
- Submitted records: Records (such as personal bests) are also retained. If you delete your account, these may be anonymised (disassociating them from your identity) but not removed entirely.
- Account data (Steam ID, Steam display name, Discord ID): Deleted when you delete your ZeepCentraal account.

## Cookies

ZeepCentraal uses only essential cookies for:

- Authentication and session management.
- Language selection.

No tracking or third-party advertising cookies are used.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the information we store about you.
- Request correction of your data.
- Unlink your Discord account at any time.
- Delete your ZeepCentraal account (subject to the data retention policy above).

To exercise these rights, please contact us at [privacy@zeepki.st](mailto:privacy@zeepki.st).

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by posting a notice on the ZeepCentraal website.

## Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [privacy@zeepki.st](mailto:privacy@zeepki.st).
