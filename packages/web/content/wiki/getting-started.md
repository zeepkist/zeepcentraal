ZeepCentraal works without game modifications for browsing levels, records, player profiles, and Super League results. Install community tools only when you want automatic record submission and ghost features.

::content-alert{type="notice" title="No mod required to browse"}
You can explore all public ZeepCentraal pages before installing Modkist or GTR.
::

## Start playing

1. Install Zeepkist through Steam.
2. Play official Adventure levels or subscribe to community Workshop levels.
3. Sign in to ZeepCentraal with Steam or Discord to personalise your dashboard.
4. Install Modkist and GTR when you want to submit records automatically.

## Enable record submission

Follow [Setup Modkist and GTR](/wiki/setup-modkist). GTR submits completed runs, keeps personal-best and world-record ghosts available, and supports bulk ghost playback for comparing racing lines.

::content-alert{type="important" title="Close Zeepkist before changing mods"}
Install, update, disable, or remove mods while the game is closed. Restart Zeepkist after every mod change.
::

## Next steps

Explore [Adventure Mode](/adventure/a), browse [community levels](/levels), follow [live records](/records), or inspect your place on the [global leaderboard](/users).

::content-alert{type="reminder" title="Check your first submission"}
After finishing a level with GTR enabled, open [your record history](/records/me) and confirm the run appears.
::
