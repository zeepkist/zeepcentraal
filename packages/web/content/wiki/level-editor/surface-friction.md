Surface physics change acceleration, braking, cornering, and recovery. Use these exact values when comparing paints or designing transitions.

| Name | Physics | Friction Front | Friction Rear | Wheel Side Slip Front | Wheel Side Slip Rear |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Everything Else | Tarmac | 70,00% | 70,00% | 20,00% | 40,00% |
| Grass/Leaves | Grass | 30,00% | 26,00% | 30,00% | 30,00% |
| Ice lvl 1 | Ice 0.15 | 15,00% | 15,00% | 100,00% | 100,00% |
| Ice lvl 2 | Ice 0.10 | 10,00% | 10,00% | 100,00% | 100,00% |
| Ice lvl 3 | Ice 0.05 | 5,00% | 5,00% | 100,00% | 100,00% |
| Mud/Flesh | Mud | 35,50% | 30,00% | 10,00% | 11,00% |
| Sand/Snow | Sand | 45,50% | 40,00% | 10,00% | 15,00% |
| Soap | Soap | 0,00% | 0,00% | 100,00% | 100,00% |
| Wood Bark | Wood | 60,00% | 60,00% | 20,00% | 40,00% |

::content-alert{type="notice" title="Everything Else uses Tarmac physics"}
Unless a surface belongs to one of the named material groups above, treat it as Tarmac for this reference.
::
