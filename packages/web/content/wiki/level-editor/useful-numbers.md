## Spawn and podium behaviour

- With multiple start blocks, players spawn on the block nearest to x0 y0 z0.
- If you do not place a podium, one spawns at x0 y500 z0. It is visible only at the end of the round.

## Road dimensions and angles

- A road piece without barriers is 16m wide, 16m long, and 1.6m high. The barriers are 1.6m high too.
- Banked roads are banked at 26.565°.
- The 32m long slope is at 14.036°.
- Off-grid turns in the off-grid folder use 10°, 15°, 30°, and 45° angles.
