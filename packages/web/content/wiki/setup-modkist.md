# Setup Modkist and GTR

Modkist is a community-built mod manager for Zeepkist. GTR is the gameplay mod that submits completed runs to ZeepCentraal and adds tools for racing against ghosts.

## What you get

- Automatic submission of completed runs to ZeepCentraal.
- Personal-best and world-record ghost replays.
- Bulk ghost playback for comparing the fastest lines through a level.
- Mod installation, updates, disabling, and removal from one manager.

## Install Modkist

1. Close Zeepkist before changing installed mods.
2. Download Modkist from [modkist.com](https://modkist.com/).
3. Follow the installer and select your Zeepkist installation when prompted.
4. Start Modkist and let it detect your game.

Modkist keeps installed mods visible and manageable. You can disable or remove a mod without reinstalling Zeepkist.

## Install GTR

1. Open Modkist.
2. Find **Zeepkist GTR** in the mod browser.
3. Install or enable GTR.
4. Launch Zeepkist through your normal Steam library.
5. Sign in when GTR prompts you to connect with ZeepCentraal.

You can also review GTR on its [official mod.io page](https://mod.io/g/zeepkist/m/zeepkist-gtr).

## Confirm record submission

Finish a level while GTR is enabled. Your run should appear on your [recent records page](/records/me) after submission. Ranked levels also show leaderboard position and point value.

## Troubleshooting

- Restart Zeepkist after installing or updating mods.
- Confirm GTR is enabled in Modkist.
- Update Modkist and GTR before investigating an older version.
- Check that security software has not quarantined Modkist files.
- Reconnect your ZeepCentraal account if submissions stop after a sign-in change.
